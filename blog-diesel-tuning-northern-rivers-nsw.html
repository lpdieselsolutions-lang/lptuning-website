Farms around Casino, Lismore and Byron Bay ask a lot of a diesel engine. Tractors put in long days slashing, baling and carting. Harvesters work flat out through short seasons. Trucks and earthmoving gear keep contractors busy across the hills and the flood plains. Diesel tuning in the Northern Rivers, done our way, means we bring the gear to your shed or paddock, so the machine never has to leave your property.

Why diesel tuning suits Northern Rivers work

A factory ECU calibration is a compromise. It has to suit every market, every grade of fuel and every type of operator. An ECU remap rewrites the fuel, boost and timing maps to suit how your machine actually works. Our headline figures are up to 18% better fuel economy and up to 30% more power. The results we have actually measured on real machines range from about 6% to 11% on fuel, and results vary by make, model and condition.

Behind every job sit 20+ years of ECU engineering and 350,000+ tunes worldwide. We work with a very experienced software engineer and deliver a tailored, customised software solution for your machine, not a generic file off the shelf. Whether you need diesel tuning in Byron Bay for a ute-based service business or tractor tuning in Casino, NSW for a cattle operation, the approach is the same.

Results from machines like yours

Tractors respond well. A John Deere 6115M went from 115 to 130 HP with an 11% fuel saving, and a New Holland T7 went from 125 to 160 HP with an 8% saving. A worked example for a Casino cattle or cane operation

Here is how the numbers can stack up on a farm tractor. This is an estimate only, using an 8% saving, which sits inside our measured range. Say the tractor burns 15 litres per hour and works 2,000 hours a year. Litres saved = 15 L/h x 2,000 hours x 8% = 2,400 litres. At diesel of $2.80 per litre, that is 2,400 x $2.80 = $6,720 a year. Your own figure depends on your machine, your load and your hours, and results vary by make, model and condition. Run the same sum with your own litres per hour and you will see quickly whether tuning stacks up.

Off-road machinery and the Reliability Solution

For off-road machinery such as tractors, harvesters, earthmoving equipment and mining machines, we also offer the Reliability Solution, which covers DPF, EGR and AdBlue removal. Emissions systems on this kind of gear can cause derates and downtime at the worst moment, like mid-harvest or mid-job. Taking them out of the equation helps keep the machine working. It is only available for off-road machinery.
