Why John Deere Machines Respond So Well to a Remap

John Deere tractors share engine families across several horsepower ratings. The same PowerTech engine can sit in a lower and a higher rated model, with the difference set largely in software. That makes John Deere machines very responsive to a tailored remap.

We remap John Deere machines across Queensland, Northern NSW and Australia-wide. Each calibration is built by a very experienced software engineer for your exact machine and engine, so the extra torque arrives where the work happens: low in the rev range, under load.

Common Questions

John Deere Tuning FAQ

Can you remap John Deere tractors?

Yes. We remap John Deere 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 series tractors, S series headers and self-propelled sprayers, from older models to Final Tier 4 machines.

How much power can a John Deere tractor gain?

It depends on the model and engine. On a John Deere 6115M we went from 115 to 130 HP (indicative). Most remaps give up to 30% more power and noticeably more torque low in the rev range.

How much fuel will a remap save on my John Deere?

On our own jobs we have measured 6% to 11%, with the best machines reaching up to 18%. Our 6115M measured 11%. Results vary by make, model and condition.

Will a remap affect my John Deere warranty?

If the tractor is still under manufacturer warranty, talk to your dealer before tuning. We will explain exactly what the remap changes so you can decide with the full picture.

Can you fix DPF or AdBlue faults on a John Deere?

For off-road John Deere machines we offer the Combo package: a remap plus our Reliability Solution for DPF, EGR and AdBlue faults, in one visit.

Do you come out to the farm?

Yes. LP Tuning is mobile and Brisbane based. We tune on the farm, in the shed or in the paddock. Travel is free within 200 km of Brisbane and we travel Australia-wide when the job makes sense.

How long does the remap take?

Usually 2 to 4 hours. We need the key and a couple of hours, power nearby is handy, and the machine is back at work the same day.

What if I am not happy with the result?

Every remap comes with a 30-day money-back trial. If you are not happy, we remove the tune and restore the original settings at no cost.
