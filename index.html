Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?
We've Got Answers

What is ECU remapping and how does it work?

ECU remapping is the process of modifying the software within your engine's Electronic Control Unit to optimise performance. We adjust injection characteristics, air-to-fuel ratios, injection duration, fuel delivery and torque limiter settings, all within safe parameters. The result is a more efficient, more powerful engine that uses less fuel for the same (or greater) work output.

Will ECU remapping void my manufacturer warranty?

ECU remapping technically sits outside standard manufacturer warranty terms, and we always recommend confirming with your dealer for your specific model. That said, most manufacturers do not proactively inspect ECU maps unless investigating a related mechanical claim, and we tune within the engine's physical tolerances. We also provide our own software warranty and a 30-day free trial period so you can test the results completely risk-free.

How much power can I expect from a remap?

Results vary by make, model and condition, but typically our customers see up to 30% more horsepower, up to 25% more torque (especially in the crucial low-to-mid RPM range), and up to 18% fuel savings. For example, a John Deere 8310R was tuned from 311hp stock to 360hp, with torque increasing from 1,452 Nm to 1,687 Nm. Every tune is custom-built for your specific machine, validated against real-world data from 350,000+ successful installations worldwide.

What brands and types of machinery do you service?

We tune virtually all makes and models. John Deere, Case IH, New Holland, CAT, Fendt, Massey Ferguson, Kubota, Deutz-Fahr, Komatsu, Kenworth, Challenger, Claas, Hitachi, Volvo, Liebherr, Kobelco, Yanmar, DAF, Scania, Western Star, Toyota, Ford, Mazda, Mitsubishi, Nissan, Isuzu, RAM, Volkswagen, Land Rover and hundreds more. We specialise in heavy equipment (agricultural, mining, construction, earthmoving) but also service trucks, cars, 4WDs and light commercial vehicles.

How does the 30-day money-back guarantee work?

It's simple. After your tune is installed, you have a full 30 days to put it to the test in real-world working conditions. If for any reason you're not completely satisfied with the performance improvements, we'll send a qualified technician to your location to remove the tune completely, at absolutely no cost to you. Your original ECU settings are restored and you walk away with zero risk.

Do you come to my location?

Yes. We provide mobile on-site service across Queensland. Whether you're on a farm, a mine site, a construction yard or a depot, we come to you with all the equipment needed. Minimal downtime for your operation is always a priority.

How long does the installation take and will it affect my operations?

Installation typically takes a couple of hours, and we come to you, whether that's on your farm, mine site or depot. We work around your schedule to minimise downtime. Most customers are back up and running the same day, with the performance difference noticeable immediately.

How much does a diesel tune cost in Queensland?

Pricing varies by machine type, make and model. Combo packages including emissions system work are also available. All tunes include a 30-day money-back trial and software warranty. Contact us with your make, model and year for an exact quote.

Is ECU tuning legal in Queensland?

Performance ECU tuning is legal in Queensland. DPF, EGR and AdBlue delete services are available for off-road machinery, agricultural equipment, and mining vehicles, not for registered on-road passenger vehicles, where emissions compliance is required by law. We only perform emissions system work on machinery that is legally permitted to be modified. Not sure where your machine sits? Contact us and we'll advise you directly.

Can you tune a Toyota Hilux or LandCruiser?

Yes: the Hilux (GUN125/GUN126) and LandCruiser (200 Series, 79 Series, 300 Series) are among the most popular vehicles we tune. Hilux owners typically gain 20–30 kW with noticeably improved torque through the mid-range, making a real difference when towing or working loaded. LandCruiser remaps are especially effective for towing performance and fuel economy on long hauls. All tunes include a 30-day trial. We service Brisbane and all of Southeast Queensland.
