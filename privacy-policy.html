4. Meta Pixel and Advertising

Our website uses the Meta Pixel (also known as the Facebook Pixel), a piece of code provided by Meta Platforms Inc. that helps us measure the effectiveness of our advertising on Facebook and Instagram, and show relevant ads to people who have previously visited our site.

What the Meta Pixel collects: When you visit our website, the Meta Pixel automatically collects certain information, which may include:

  • Pages you visit and actions you take (such as viewing a service page or submitting an enquiry form)
  • Your IP address, browser type, and operating system
  • Cookie identifiers that allow Meta to recognise your browser across sessions
  • Referral information (how you arrived at our site)

How this data is used: This information is shared with Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd and may be used to:

  • Measure whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram led to enquiries or website visits
  • Show our ads to people who have visited our website (retargeting)
  • Build audiences of people similar to our existing customers (lookalike audiences)

Overseas disclosure: Data collected by the Meta Pixel is transmitted to and stored on Meta's servers, which are located outside Australia (including in the United States and Ireland). By using our website, you acknowledge that your data may be processed overseas. Meta is required to handle this data in accordance with their Data Policy.

You can review Meta's Data Policy at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

How to opt out: You can limit how Meta uses your data for advertising purposes by:

  • Visiting your Facebook Ad Preferences and adjusting your settings
  • Using the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool
  • Disabling cookies in your browser settings (note: this may affect website functionality)

If you have questions about how the Meta Pixel handles your data, please contact us at info@lptuning.com.au.

5. Google Ads and Google Analytics

Our website uses Google Ads conversion tracking and, where enabled, Google Analytics. These are services provided by Google LLC and Google Ireland Ltd that help us measure the effectiveness of our advertising and understand how visitors use our website.

What Google collects: When you visit our website or interact with our Google Ads, Google may collect:

  • Pages you visit and actions you take (such as submitting an enquiry form)
  • Your IP address, browser type, and device information
  • Cookie identifiers and advertising IDs used to recognise your browser or device
  • Click and conversion data from our Google Ads campaigns

How this data is used: This information is shared with Google and may be used to:

  • Measure whether our Google Ads led to enquiries or website visits (conversion tracking)
  • Show our ads to people who have previously visited our website (remarketing)
  • Improve and optimise our advertising campaigns
  • Provide aggregated analytics about website usage (if Google Analytics is enabled)

Overseas disclosure: Data collected via Google tools is transmitted to and stored on Google's servers, which may be located outside Australia (including in the United States). Google is required to handle this data in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable data protection law.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

How to opt out: You can limit how Google uses your data for advertising by:

  • Visiting Google Ads Settings and adjusting your personalisation preferences
  • Installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Using the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool
  • Disabling cookies in your browser settings (this may affect website functionality)

What is stored: The details you submit through our forms, including your name, phone number, machine type, location, and any other information you provide, are sent to and stored in HighLevel.

How we use it: We use HighLevel to:

  • Respond to your service enquiry

  • Send relevant follow-up messages about our services (you can opt out at any time)

Overseas disclosure: HighLevel is a US-based platform. Data you submit through our forms may be stored on servers located in the United States. HighLevel is required to handle your data in accordance with applicable data protection law. You can review HighLevel's privacy practices at gohighlevel.com/privacy-policy.

To request deletion of your data from our CRM, please contact us at info@lptuning.com.au.
