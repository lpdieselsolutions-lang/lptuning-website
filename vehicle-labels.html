Label Finder Guide

Where to Find the Labels

Every vehicle and machine is different. Select your equipment type below for specific guidance on where to look.

ECU Label: Open the engine bonnet. The ECU is usually a rectangular black box mounted to the firewall, engine bay side panel, or under the cab floor. The label is printed on top of the ECU casing and shows a part number, serial number, and software ID.

  • John Deere: right-hand side of engine bay near the firewall
  • Case IH / New Holland: under the cab floor panel or right-hand engine compartment, on New Holland, also check underneath the AdBlue cover
  • Fendt: inside the engine bay, left-hand side near the battery
  • Massey Ferguson: right-hand engine bay or behind the cab access panel
  • Deutz-Fahr: usually visible in the engine bay when the bonnet is fully open

Engine Label: Look on the engine block itself. It is usually stamped or on a metal plate attached to the side or top of the block. It shows the engine serial number, displacement, and model code.

  • Typically on the left or right side of the engine block
  • Sometimes on the valve cover or injection pump housing
  • May be on a metal tag wired to the engine
Take photos in good light with the labels clean and dry. If needed, gently wipe with a rag first. The full part number must be readable.

ECU Label: Access the engine compartment by opening the rear service panel or lifting the engine hood. The ECU is typically mounted away from heat on a bracket near the engine bay wall or under the cab.

  • Komatsu: right-hand engine bay behind the access panel
  • CAT (Caterpillar): inside the engine compartment on the right-hand wall
  • Hitachi: rear engine bay, upper right area
  • Volvo: near the battery or behind the cab access panel
  • Liebherr: usually on the engine bay firewall

Engine Label: Found on the engine block or attached tag. Open the service panel fully for access.

  • Side of the engine block near the front
  • On the timing case cover or injection pump bracket
  • Stamped directly onto the block (may need cleaning)
A torch helps a lot in tight engine bays. Use your phone's camera with flash on rather than trying to read it by eye.

ECU Label: On most trucks the ECU is located inside the cab under the dashboard, behind the passenger kick panel, or in the engine bay near the firewall.

  • Kenworth / Peterbilt: behind passenger side kick panel or under the dashboard
  • Volvo FH/FM: under the cab steps or in the engine bay left-hand side
  • Mercedes Actros: engine bay right-hand firewall
  • Isuzu / Mitsubishi Fuso: under the cab or engine bay firewall
  • DAF: engine bay, left-hand side near the battery box

Engine Label: On or near the engine block. Pop the bonnet or access from the cab tilt (on cab-over models).

  • Front-left or front-right of the engine block
  • On the rocker cover or a bracket attached to the block
  • Also check the compliance plate in the engine bay for additional info
On cab-over trucks, tilt the cab to access the engine bay fully. Make sure the engine is cool before reaching in.

ECU Label: Open the bonnet. On most modern 4WDs the ECU is a sealed black or silver box, often in a plastic housing.

  • Toyota LandCruiser / HiLux: firewall, passenger side or behind the battery
  • Ford Ranger / Everest: passenger side firewall or engine bay bulkhead
  • Nissan Patrol / Navara: behind the battery or passenger side firewall
  • Mitsubishi Triton / Pajero: engine bay, firewall area
  • Isuzu D-Max / MU-X: behind the battery on the driver's side

Engine Label: Usually on a sticker or metal plate on the engine block, visible when you open the bonnet.

  • Top of the engine block or on the rocker cover
  • Side of the block near the front
  • Also check the compliance plate on the firewall for engine code
Can't find the ECU? Check your vehicle owner's manual or search "[make/model year] ECU location" online. If still unsure, just send us your vehicle details and we can guide you over the phone.

ECU Label: Usually located in the engine bay or a dedicated electronics/service panel inside the cab. Some models have the ECU behind a removable panel in the engine access area.

  • John Deere S/T Series: engine compartment, right-hand side or service platform
  • Case IH Axial-Flow: engine bay service panel or cab electronics box
  • New Holland: similar to Case IH, check the engine bay and cab area
  • Claas Lexion: rear engine bay, accessible from the service platform

Engine Label: On the engine block, accessible from the engine service platform or by opening the rear engine access door.
