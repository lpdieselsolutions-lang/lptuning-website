Truck & Fleet Tuning:
Where Fuel Savings Scale

For transport operators and fleet owners, fuel is typically the second-largest operating expense after driver wages. A modern semi-trailer or B-double consuming 50 to 60 litres per 100km, running 200,000+ kilometres per year, spends $180,000 to $220,000 on diesel annually. A 15% reduction in fuel consumption saves $27,000 to $33,000 per truck, per year.

LP Tuning provides ECU remapping for trucks, heavy transport, and commercial vehicle fleets throughout Queensland. Whether you operate a single prime mover or a fleet of B-doubles, the return on investment calculation is straightforward. And unlike most business investments, the benefit compounds every year the truck runs.

Power When You Need It

A remapped truck produces more torque in the mid-range, which is where road haulage actually operates. Better pull on hills, faster acceleration out of intersections, and improved ability to maintain highway speed under load mean less gearchanging, less drivetrain stress, and a more confident drive.

Lower Fuel Cost Per Km

The remap optimises combustion efficiency. More work is extracted from each litre of diesel, and the engine operates at lower load percentages for a given speed and payload. The result is measurable fuel savings in real highway and regional driving conditions.

Common Questions

Truck Tuning FAQ

Will a remap affect my ADR compliance or roadworthiness?

Our remap adjusts ECU software parameters and does not alter physical engine components. We operate within the engine's safety margins. For specific ADR questions relevant to your fleet, we recommend consulting your transport compliance advisor.

Can you service our trucks at our depot?

Yes. We come to you, whether that's a depot, a freight yard, or a roadside location in Queensland. No need to take the truck off-route or to a workshop. Each truck typically takes 2 to 4 hours.

Do you offer fleet discounts?

Yes. For fleets of five or more vehicles, we offer volume pricing. Contact us to discuss your fleet size and we'll provide a tailored quote.

Is there a difference between remapping a European truck (Volvo, Scania) vs an American (Kenworth, Western Star)?

The process is the same, but the ECU architecture and tune profiles differ significantly. European trucks tend to have more conservative factory derating and often see larger relative gains. We have data and proven profiles for both platforms.
