The Challenge

Mining Equipment Demands
Maximum Uptime

In mining and construction, downtime is not measured in inconvenience. It is measured in dollars. A large excavator or dozer generating $1,500 to $5,000 of productive value per shift cannot afford to be parked for emission system maintenance, DPF regeneration cycles, or AdBlue-triggered shutdowns.

Yet that is exactly what modern Tier 4 emission regulations have introduced to heavy construction and mining equipment. Systems designed for passenger vehicles and urban air quality have been mandated onto machines that operate at sustained high loads in dusty, remote, and extreme-temperature environments. The result is predictable: failure rates, maintenance costs, and unscheduled downtime that undermine the economics of the operation.

The Cost of Emission Downtime

Every hour a machine is offline for DPF regeneration, AdBlue fault clearance, or EGR maintenance is an hour of lost production. For machines operating around the clock, these interruptions compound quickly. Proactive management of emission systems through delete and remap eliminates this cost permanently.

Performance Under Sustained Load

Mining and earthmoving equipment operates at or near full load for extended periods. At these loads, DPF backpressure and EGR-related derating directly reduce productive output. A properly remapped machine maintains full power under load, improving cycle times and throughput.

Common Questions

Mining Tuning FAQ

Can you service equipment at remote mine sites?

Yes. We are fully mobile and travel throughout Queensland. For remote locations, we can discuss logistics and travel arrangements during the consultation. We have experience servicing machines at remote agricultural and resource sector sites.

Can you tune multiple machines at a site visit?

Yes, and we encourage this for cost efficiency. Scheduling multiple machines in a single visit reduces the per-machine cost of travel. Contact us to discuss your fleet requirements.

Is the DPF/EGR/AdBlue delete legal for our mine site machinery?

Emission system deletes are legal for off-road machinery used in mining, construction, and agriculture. We recommend confirming with your compliance team for site-specific requirements. The modifications are performed on the understanding that the machines are operated as off-road mining equipment.

What is the turnaround time per machine?

A standard ECU remap takes 2 to 4 hours per machine. A full Combo service (delete + remap) takes 4 to 6 hours. We can often complete multiple machines per day visit, depending on the make and complexity.
