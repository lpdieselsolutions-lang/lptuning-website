How the math works

The 8% Rule

The conservative baseline we use for estimates:

Trucks: km/yr ÷ 100 × L/100km × 8% × $/L
Machinery: L/hr × annual hrs × 8% × $/L

Common questions

FAQ

What exactly does the 30-day trial mean?
After we install your tune, you run the machine normally for 30 days. If at any point during that period you're not seeing the results you expected, or you simply want to go back to stock, we come back and revert the ECU to the factory file. There is no charge for the revert and no hidden conditions. We want you to feel the difference before you commit.
How long does the tune take?
Most machines are done in two to four hours on-site. We read the existing ECU file, calibrate the new map to your specific machine and operating conditions, flash the ECU, and do a final check before we leave. Downtime is minimal.
Will it affect my manufacturer warranty?
ECU remapping does sit outside standard manufacturer warranty terms, as it is a modification to the factory software. However, most heavy machinery in Australia is past the manufacturer warranty period by the time owners consider a tune. If your machine is still under warranty, we recommend discussing this with your dealer first. We can revert to stock at any time if a warranty claim arises.
Is it legal for on-road trucks?
Yes. For all on-road heavy vehicles, we work within the engine's defined parameters and do not interfere with any emissions control systems (DPF, EGR, SCR/AdBlue). The vehicle remains road-legal and emissions compliant under Australian standards. We do not offer emissions system removal for on-road vehicles.
Can I get a tune on a car or ute?
This check is for heavy diesel machinery only — trucks, tractors, excavators, loaders, dozers, and similar. Got a ute or 4WD? Give us a call on 0491 097 706 and we'll help point you in the right direction.
